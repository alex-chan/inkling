Markets brace for a long winter as tariff talks stall

By Elena Hart, economics correspondent · 6 min read

Global markets braced for further turbulence on Tuesday after negotiators failed to break the deadlock over tariffs, leaving exporters to grapple with mounting uncertainty.

Officials refused to rule out fresh levies, and analysts warned that the protracted dispute could exacerbate an already sluggish recovery. In the wake of the talks, several manufacturers said they would postpone investment until the outlook became less opaque.

“Nobody wants an escalation,” said one veteran trade lawyer, “but the rhetoric on both sides has become remarkably entrenched.”

Shipping firms, meanwhile, reported that bookings for the spring season had fallen sharply, a sign that companies are hedging against a prolonged standoff.
