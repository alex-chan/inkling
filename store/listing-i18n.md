# 商店介绍：English / 繁體中文

名称和简短说明来自 `_locales/*/messages.json`，上传 1.0.2 后两个商店会自动按语言显示。下面是各语言的「详细说明」：在 Chrome 后台「商品详情」右上角切换语言后粘贴；在 Edge 的「Store listings」里点「Add languages」后粘贴。简体中文仍用 [listing.md](listing.md) 里的版本。

## English (en)

Reading English news or watching English videos and keep hitting words you don't know or can't pronounce? Inkling glosses only the words above your vocabulary level with short Chinese meanings, and leaves the rest of the page untouched.

[Supported sites]
Runs by default on major English news and video sites: CNN, NBC News, CNBC, Reuters, BBC, Google News, The Wall Street Journal, YouTube (including captions), The New York Times, The Guardian, AP, Bloomberg, The Washington Post, Financial Times, The Economist, NPR, Al Jazeera, ABC News, CBS News, Fox News, Politico, Axios, The Atlantic, The New Yorker, TIME, USA Today, Forbes and Business Insider. Enable any other site with one click from the toolbar popup.

[Glosses matched to your level]
• Pick a level (junior high to GRE) or slide to any value; a 40-word quiz estimates your vocabulary
• Three display styles: small text above the word, in brackets, or underline only; each word is glossed once per paragraph
• Understands word forms (tariffs → tariff, went → go) and skips names, acronyms, navigation, code and input fields
• Known and learning lists: mark a word as known and it's never glossed again; learning words are always glossed

[Word cards with US IPA and audio]
• Click a glossed word for a card with US IPA, Chinese definitions, frequency rank and exam tags
• One-click pronunciation with your choice of voice and speed, or play it automatically
• Select any word or phrase and click the 注 button to look it up

[Optional AI]
• "Explain in context": what the word means in this sentence, how it's pronounced and used
• "AI-optimized glosses": re-decides what each paragraph needs for your level, gives in-context meanings, adds uncommon senses (brace in "markets brace for…") and idioms (in the wake of), and labels CEFR levels
• Connect with any OpenAI-compatible API key, or sign in with your ChatGPT account (uses your Plus / Pro plan)

[Interface languages]
简体中文, 繁體中文 and English. Glosses are in Chinese.

[Privacy]
The dictionary works offline. The developer runs no servers and collects no data; text is sent only to the AI provider you choose, and only when you use AI features. Open source: github.com/alex-chan/inkling

## 繁體中文 (zh_TW)

讀英文新聞、看英文影片時，總有幾個詞不認識、不會唸？Inkling 依你的詞彙量，只為超出你程度的生詞加上中文註釋，其餘原文一字不動。

【支援的網站】
預設在這些英文新聞和影片網站上執行：CNN、NBC News、CNBC、Reuters、BBC、Google 新聞、華爾街日報、YouTube（含影片字幕）、紐約時報、衛報、AP、Bloomberg、華盛頓郵報、金融時報、經濟學人、NPR、半島電視台、ABC News、CBS News、Fox News、Politico、Axios、The Atlantic、紐約客、TIME、USA Today、Forbes、Business Insider。
其他網站可在工具列彈出視窗中點「在此網站啟用」，個別授權。

【依程度註釋生詞】
• 詞彙程度可選：國中／高中／四級／六級／考研／雅思托福／專八／GRE，也可用滑桿自由調整；提供 40 詞小測驗，幫你估算詞彙量
• 註釋方式三選一：詞上方小字、詞後括註、只加底線；同一段中同一個詞只在第一次出現時顯示釋義
• 自動還原詞形（tariffs → tariff、went → go），自動略過人名地名、縮寫、導覽列、程式碼和輸入框
• 熟詞本、生詞本：點「我認識這個詞」後不再註釋；加入生詞本的詞一律註釋

【詞卡：釋義、美式音標、發音】
• 點擊被註釋的詞，跳出詞卡：美式 IPA 音標、中文釋義、詞頻排名和考試標籤
• 🔊 一鍵朗讀，可選擇聲音、調整語速，也可設定開啟詞卡時自動朗讀
• 選取任意詞或片語，點「注」按鈕即可查詢

【接入大型語言模型（選用）】
• 「AI 語境解釋」：結合所在句子說明這個詞在本句的意思、讀音、用法和背景
• 「AI 優化註釋」：依你的程度重新判斷每段該註釋什麼，釋義依上下文給出；補上熟詞僻義（如 markets brace for… 中的 brace 表示「準備應對」）和慣用語（如 in the wake of），並標出 CEFR 難度等級
• 兩種接入方式：填寫 OpenAI 相容介面的 API Key，或使用 ChatGPT 帳號登入（消耗 Plus / Pro 方案用量）
• 介面為繁體中文時，AI 釋義也以繁體中文輸出（離線詞典為簡體）

【介面語言】
简体中文、繁體中文、English。

【隱私】
離線詞典，不連網也能註釋。開發者不經營伺服器、不蒐集任何資料；只有在你使用 AI 功能時，相關文字才會直接傳送到你自己選擇的模型服務。開放原始碼：github.com/alex-chan/inkling
